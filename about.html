02 What interests me

Three areas I want to explore.

Business ideas & implementation

I enjoy exploring business ideas and thinking about how to put them into practice. I'm interested in understanding a business need, developing a possible solution, and planning how it could work in the real world.

Website & UI/UX design

I'm interested in designing websites that are visually appealing and easy to use. I want to explore how layouts, user flows, and thoughtful interface choices can make a digital experience feel clear and intuitive.

I'm curious about how AI can help businesses work more effectively, from simplifying repetitive tasks to supporting decisions. I want to understand where AI is useful and how it can be applied to a real business problem.
